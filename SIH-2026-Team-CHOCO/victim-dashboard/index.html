Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to process your complaint:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and physical address.
  • Fraud Details: Amount lost, date/time of fraud, type of fraud, and description.
  • Fraudster Information: UPI IDs, bank account numbers, and phone numbers provided by you.
  • Evidence: Screenshots, documents, and other files you upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Complaint Processing: To register and track your cybercrime complaint.
  • Investigation: To share with law enforcement agencies for investigation.
  • Communication: To send you updates about your complaint status.
  • AI Analysis: To analyze patterns and predict fraud hotspots (anonymized data).

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access Control: Only authorized law enforcement personnel can access complaint data.
  • Audit Trail: All access to your data is logged and auditable.
  • Blockchain: Evidence hashes are stored on blockchain for tamper-proof verification.

4. Data Sharing

Your information may be shared with:

  • Law Enforcement: Police departments and cybercrime cells investigating your complaint.
  • Banks: Financial institutions to freeze accounts and recover funds.
  • Regulatory Bodies: RBI, SEBI, and other regulatory authorities as required by law.
  • We never sell your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements).
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

6. Contact Us

For privacy-related queries, contact our Data Protection Officer:
